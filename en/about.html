About the project

Who wrote it, who organized it and how this book site was made.

Author of the content

Luciano Carlos Cunha

A Brazilian philosopher, he holds a PhD in Ethics and Political Philosophy from the Federal University of Santa Catarina, is the general coordinator in Brazil of the activities of the organization Animal Ethics, and created the website Senciência e Ética. He is also the author of the books Uma breve introdução à ética animal: desde as questões clássicas até o que vem sendo discutido atualmente (A Brief Introduction to Animal Ethics: From the Classic Questions to Current Debates) and Razões para ajudar: o sofrimento dos animais selvagens e suas implicações éticas (Reasons to Help: The Suffering of Wild Animals and Its Ethical Implications). He has also published chapters in other books and articles in specialized journals. Learn more on the author's Wikipedia page (in Portuguese).

Luciano provided the complete content of the 12 volumes of the collection A Journey Through Animal Ethics: From Basic to Advanced and authorized this project.

Project organization

Julio Cesar Prava

He is a graphic designer and content curator. Vegan for more than 10 years, he founded the Vegpedia project. He currently lives in Brisbane, Australia. He organized the structure of this book site. He is also the organizer of BiblioVerse, a platform with many useful libraries on a variety of subjects, which also runs special projects such as Ética Para Iniciantes, Política Para Iniciantes, Ciência Para Iniciantes, the Guia de Argumentação pelos Animais and the Ethical Food Guide.

The collection

A Journey Through Animal Ethics: From Basic to Advanced aims to offer, in texts that are accessible yet in-depth, an analysis of the many questions debated in the field of animal ethics from its beginnings to the present day. The 12 volumes were published in 2025, in open access, and their production was funded by the organization Animal Ethics. The original PDFs (in Portuguese) are available on the Senciência e Ética website.

How the site was made

  • Original text preserved. The text of the 12 volumes was extracted from the PDFs and checked against the tables of contents: all 186 chapters, the 1,219 notes and the 1,198 references. This English edition is a complete translation of that text, made for this site; the Portuguese original is on the main site.
  • Linked sources. Every citation in author (year) format becomes a link to the full reference in the volume. The links the author included to other volumes of the collection lead to the corresponding chapter on this site.
  • Context. On the first mention of each author in a chapter, one click shows who the person is and what they argue, so readers can understand the role of the citation in the discussion.
  • Study layers. Summaries, explanations for kids, analogies, arguments in logical form, questions and tools were written for this site, based on the text. They are always marked as study layers and can be hidden with “Original only”.

How to help

  • Spread the word: share the texts, the tools and the Debunking page with anyone interested in ethics.
  • Teach: use the chapters, the layers for kids and the quizzes in the classroom or in study groups. Everything is free.
  • Correct: let us know about transcription errors, misplaced notes or study layers that don't reflect the text well.
  • Support those who produce the knowledge: the collection was funded by the organization Animal Ethics, which welcomes donations and volunteers.

Corrections

If you find a transcription error, a misplaced note or a study layer that doesn't reflect the text well, write to BiblioVerse stating the volume, the chapter and the passage.

Privacy

The site uses no accounts, ads or tracking. Your progress, your highlights and your reading preferences are saved only in your browser. Details in the privacy policy.

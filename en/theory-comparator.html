Ethical theory comparator

Choose the theories and compare, side by side, what each one says about animals, exploitation and wild animals.

Study layer · written for this site based on the text by Luciano Carlos Cunha

The conclusion of Volume XI: the theories disagree about a lot, but there is a minimal degree of convergence. Whichever theory is most plausible, there are strong reasons to consider all sentient beings. Those that pursue a goal imply equal consideration; the others are, at the very least, compatible with it.

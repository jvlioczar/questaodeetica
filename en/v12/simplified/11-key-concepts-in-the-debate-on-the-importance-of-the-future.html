Key Concepts in the Debate on the Importance of the Future

In plain language, for readers who are new to the topic. About 2 min of reading.

What is this page? A simplified explanation written for this site based on Luciano Carlos Cunha’s text. It leaves out details, examples and sources. For the full argument, read the author’s original text.

What s-risks are

New word

S-risks (risks of suffering): risks that, in the future, things happen that cause gigantic suffering, more than all the suffering that has ever existed on Earth.

Today, many animals suffer in ways that didn't exist in the past, like in factory farming. Something similar, or much worse, could appear in the future.

S-risks and x-risks

  • X-risks are risks of humanity going extinct.
  • S-risks are risks of enormous suffering.

They're not the same: humanity could survive and still cause a lot of suffering.

How to measure how serious a risk is

How serious an s-risk is gets calculated by multiplying how bad the scenario would be by the chance that it happens. The result is called expected disvalue. So even an unlikely scenario can be very serious, if it's gigantic.

Examples of s-risks

  • spreading factory farming to more countries and species;
  • exploiting insects on a massive scale;
  • creating digital minds that can feel, with no protection;
  • taking ecosystems full of suffering to other planets.

Substratism

New word

Substratism: discriminating against someone because of what their body is made of, for example whether it's organic or digital.

Just as speciesism discriminates by species, substratism would discriminate against beings that can feel just because they aren't made of flesh and blood.

Remember

The future could bring suffering on a scale never seen before. Thinking about it today is a form of protection.

In short

  • S-risks are risks of gigantic suffering in the future.
  • How serious a risk is equals its size times its probability.
  • Substratism would mean discriminating against beings that can feel because they aren't organic.

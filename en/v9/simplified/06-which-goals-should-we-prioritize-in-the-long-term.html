Which Goals Should We Prioritize in the Long Term?

In plain language, for readers who are new to the topic. About 2 min of reading.

What is this page? A simplified explanation written for this site based on Luciano Carlos Cunha’s text. It leaves out details, examples and sources. For the full argument, read the author’s original text.

On this page

  1. Different goals for the future
  2. Two factors
  3. How they combine
  4. What this shows

Different goals for the future

People who want to have the best impact on the future can have different goals:

  • preventing human extinction;
  • preventing suffering;
  • preventing early deaths;
  • increasing happiness;
  • and others.

Someone might want all of them, but to different degrees. How do we decide how much to focus on each one?

Two factors

To decide how much to focus on preventing suffering, it depends on two factors:

  • Weight: how much importance we give to preventing suffering compared to other goals.
  • Expectation: how much suffering we think the future could hold.

How they combine

  • Someone who's very optimistic about the future has to give a lot of weight to preventing suffering to conclude that it should be the focus.
  • Someone who thinks the future could hold a lot of suffering doesn't need to give it as much weight to reach the same conclusion.

This can be summed up in a simple formula:

Degree of focus on suffering = Weight × Expectation

New word

Expectation: how likely we think something is to happen and how big it would be.

What this shows

Even people with different values can agree that it's worth focusing on preventing future suffering, if they think it could be very large.

Remember

The more suffering the future might bring, the more sense it makes to focus on preventing it, whatever our main goal is.

In short

  • There are several possible goals for the future.
  • How much we focus on suffering depends on the weight we give it and how much we think could happen.
  • Those who expect a lot of suffering have strong reasons to make it a priority.

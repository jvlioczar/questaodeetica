About

Privacy policy

In short: there is no sign-up, and we use no first-party cookies, advertising or visitor analytics tools.

This page explains what data may be processed when you use Questão de Ética. It is in line with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018) and follows general data-protection principles, such as data minimization and transparency.

Last updated: November 6, 2026.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to sign up, log in or pay.
  • There is no form that sends your data to us.
  • We don't use first-party cookies, advertising or visitor analytics tools.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.

What stays in your browser

Some features use your device's local storage (localStorage). This information is not sent to us:

FeatureWhat it stores
Mark as readThe list of texts you have marked
Highlights and notesThe passages you highlighted and what you wrote about them
Reading preferencesThe font size and the “Original only” mode
Quizzes and toolsSome answers, so you can pick up where you left off

To delete them, use “Delete highlights” in My Journey or clear this site's data in your browser settings. On shared devices, other people may be able to see these marks.

Listening to the text

The “Listen” button uses your browser's built-in voice. Depending on the browser, speech synthesis may be handled by the vendor's service (for example, Google or Microsoft), which receives the passage being read. We don't send anything to these services ourselves.

Third-party services

As with any website, your browser sends technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to the services that deliver the pages and files:

ServiceWhat for
Site hostingDelivering the pages. The provider may keep technical access logs
Google FontsLoading the site's fonts
BiblioVerseLoading the top bar with the BiblioVerse libraries

External links

We point to works, organizations and other websites. When you visit them, their own privacy rules apply.

Children and teenagers

The site is educational and does not ask anyone, of any age, for personal data.

Your rights

The LGPD, like data-protection laws generally, guarantees rights such as access to, correction of and deletion of personal data. Since we keep no accounts and don't store what you do here, we generally have no data that could identify you. What is on your device stays under your control.

Changes

If the way the site works changes, for example with new tools or services, this page will be updated with a new date.

Debate simulator

Someone raises an objection. Choose the best reply, see why it works and read the author's full reply.

The objections and the full replies come from the original text (the Volume XII boxes). The summary of the right reply and the weak alternatives were written for this site. One of the wrong alternatives is a good reply, but to a different objection: in a debate, answering what was actually said is half the work.
